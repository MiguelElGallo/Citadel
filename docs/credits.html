Credits and licenses

Citadel Atlas is an independent guide to Microsoft's four-layer Citadel architecture. Miguel P Z created the explorer, planning notes, and workbook.

Architecture and service data

The service roles and landing zones come from Microsoft's blueprint. The explorer links each service use to its sources. The connection details link to the document or implementation that describes the two endpoints.
